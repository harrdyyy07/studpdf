VTU 2022 Scheme Passing Marks & Rules Explained

By vtuwise Team • Published on Oct 24, 2023 • 4 min read

If you've recently joined a VTU-affiliated engineering college under the 2022 scheme, navigating the exam and grading system can be overwhelming. Visvesvaraya Technological University (VTU) frequently updates its evaluation schemes to maintain academic rigor.

The 2022 Scheme introduces specific thresholds for Continuous Internal Evaluation (CIE) and Semester End Examination (SEE) that every student must understand to avoid gaining unwanted backlog subjects.

The Core Rule: 50% vs 35% vs 40%

Unlike some older schemes, the 2022 scheme places a much stronger emphasis on both internal and external consistency. Here is the absolute baseline rule you must memorize:

  • CIE (Internals): You must secure a minimum of 50% (25 out of 50 marks) in your internals to even be eligible to write the final exam.
  • SEE (Externals): You must secure a minimum of 35% (18 out of 50 marks) in the final written exam to pass the external component.
  • Total Combined: Your combined CIE + SEE marks must be at least 40% (40 out of 100) to pass the subject.

Subject Breakdown (100 Marks Distribution)

For a standard 3-credit or 4-credit subject, VTU conducts a 100-mark final exam, which is later scaled down to 50 marks. Your internals are conducted for 50 marks.

Component Maximum Marks Passing Minimum
Continuous Internal Evaluation (CIE) 50 Marks 25 Marks (50%)
Semester End Exam (SEE) - Scaled 50 Marks 18 Marks (35%)
Total Passing Requirement 100 Marks 40 Marks (40%)

What Happens If You Fail Internals?

If you fail to secure the mandatory 25 marks in your CIE (internals), you will be flagged as NSSR (Not Satisfied Sessional Requirements). This means you will not be issued a hall ticket for that specific subject, and you cannot write the main exam.

To clear this, you will have to re-register for the subject when it is offered again, re-attend classes, and re-write the internal assessments. Always take your internals seriously!

The "18 is not always enough" Trap

A common mistake students make is calculating their pass marks blindly. While the minimum SEE requirement is 18/50, getting exactly 18 means you need to have secured at least 22 marks in your internals to hit the 40/100 total passing requirement.

If you got 25 in your internals (the bare minimum), you must score at least 15/50 in your final exam to pass. Wait, the rule says minimum 18! Therefore, scoring 25 in internals means you still need an 18 in the final, which gives you 43/100 (Pass).

How to Prepare?

Passing under the 2022 scheme is incredibly manageable if you use the right resources. Relying solely on vast textbooks right before the exam is a recipe for disaster.

  1. Solve PYQs: Start by solving Previous Year Question Papers (PYQs). VTU is known to repeat themes and question formats.
  2. Use Quality Notes: Use focused, module-wise notes rather than dense textbooks in the last few weeks of prep.
